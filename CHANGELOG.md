# DinoGame design changes

## v3 — October 12, 2026
- **Removed** proposed analysis goggles/scan previews of which team member can earn a particular skill. Opponent learning compatibility must stay secret.
- **Reinforced** that one-time victories irreversibly consume learning opportunities for every other dinosaur in the run, and outcomes are seeded and non-guaranteed.
- **Separated** intrinsic opponent formidability, winning dinosaur-relative threat and broad species compatibility as independent skill-reward influences.
- **Changed balance philosophy:** large dinosaurs should be dramatically stronger in direct fights; small species retain superior agility, attack speed, dodging/stamina efficiency and unique exploration access.
- **Specified** Ankylosaurus armored regions resist ordinary hits, countered by rare named Piercing (accurate/less damaging) or Crushing (high damage/less accurate and/or effort-intensive) moves. Hitbox details still open.
- **Confirmed** fixed island geography, constrained variable encounters plus always-occurring scripted encounters.
- **Removed** permanent gameplay upgrades/unlocked species. Only an informational journal with observed species, skills and storyline persists; all power, team members and DNA reset between runs.
- **Deferred** Parasaurolophus special sensing to later discussion; never permit it to spoil skill learnability.
- **Maintained** restricted Phase 0–1 Codex implementation: just a two-dinosaur real-time combat prototype.

## v2 — October 11, 2026
Earlier exploratory draft used scanner goggles and broader persistent unlocks. Its conflicting concepts are superseded by v3.
