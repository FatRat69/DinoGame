# Change log

## v2 — October 11, 2026
- Added an optional, findable **analysis goggles** item that indicates **which team dinosaur has a chance to learn from a scanned opponent**, without guaranteeing a skill reward.
- Clarified **size trade-offs**: smaller dinosaurs tend toward **stamina efficiency, faster attacks, improved dodging and agility**, and the player actively chooses **Rest** between moves; upgrades can improve dinosaur stats.
- Replaced all prior ideas for procedurally rearranging/rebuilding island geography: **world remains fixed**, encounter quantities, placement and compositions vary by seeded run.
- Defined persistent species discoveries, story knowledge, scan knowledge and selected long-term unlocks; **run roster, skills and DNA reset**. Permanent stat upgrades remain a balance-sensitive later decision.
- Kept 12 playable dinosaur species and two nonplayable prehistoric reptiles; combat remains real time and command-based.
- First Codex task remains intentionally only the basic **Phase 0–1 combat prototype**.
